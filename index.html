Before anyone sends money, three things everyone should know

  • One PAN can submit only one application per IPO. Duplicate applications on the same PAN get rejected, so a pool cannot multiply anyone's chances of allotment by applying many times under one name.
  • Shares land in one person's demat account. Whoever applies legally holds them. Write down what everyone has agreed, in plain language, and keep a copy.
  • Pooling other people's money to invest carries rules. In India that can bring in SEBI regulation depending on how it is run. Worth an hour with a chartered accountant before the first rupee moves. This page is a record book, not advice — I'm not a financial adviser or a lawyer.
