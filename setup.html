Set up passphrases

This runs entirely on your own computer. Nothing you type here is sent anywhere.

1 · Your current file

Broken or working, either is fine — it gets rebuilt.

2 · Two new passphrases

Make them different from each other, and different from anything you use elsewhere. Three unrelated words is easy to say over the phone and hard to guess.

3 · Keeping your existing records

Fill this in if the file already holds investors and IPOs you want to keep — they get carried across and re-encrypted under the new passphrase. Leave it blank only if the file is broken or empty.

If you leave box 3 blank, the ledger starts empty. Open the rebuilt site, enter the viewing passphrase, unlock editing with the owner one, and add the real investors and IPOs there.
